COMBATING CYBERCRIME

Educational Suite

Module 1

Core Vocabulary

Learn the basic cybersecurity terminology needed to discuss the topic. Includes interactive flashcards, British English pronunciation, spelling practice, and a word-matching game.

Study the types of cybercrimes, their English definitions, Russian equivalents, and pronunciation. Then test yourself in a timed term-and-definition matching game.

Qualify 20 cyber incident reports under time pressure, then build grammatically correct police reports using passive voice constructions.

Module 4

A competitive team-based review game. Answer conceptual and lexical questions correctly to secure fields on the grid and link five points in a row.
